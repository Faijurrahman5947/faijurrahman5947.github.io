I'm currently a PhD student at the Department of Astronomy, Tsinghua University, under the supervision of Prof. We Cui. I received my bachelor's degree in physics from Nankai University in 2021.

My research focuses on three closely connected areas: (1) Astronomical instrumentation: developing detector systems, simulating sky surveys, and preparing data-analysis methods for the DIffuse X-ray Explorer (DIXE); (2) Stellar feedback and galactic ecosystems: studying how superbubbles redistribute energy and metals, interact with their surroundings, and influence star formation; (3) High-resolution X-ray spectroscopy: working with grating spectra and developing techniques for TES microcalorimeter spectroscopy to probe the physical conditions and chemical composition of hot gas. My research uses observations from XMM-Newton, Chandra, and Einstein Probe.
